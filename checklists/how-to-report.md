# How to report a suspicious message

> Replace the bracketed items with your organization's details.

```text
 Suspicious message
        │
        ▼
 Did you click, open, or reply? ──── No ───► Report it using [REPORT BUTTON / reports@your-org.example]
        │                                      then delete it.
       Yes
        ▼
 Did you enter a password or code? ── Yes ──► 1. Change the password from a trusted device.
        │                                       2. Call [SECURITY HOTLINE] now.
        No                                      3. Tell us what you entered and when.
        ▼
 Did you open an attachment / install? ─ Yes ► 1. Disconnect from the network (Wi-Fi off / unplug).
        │                                       2. Call [SECURITY HOTLINE]. Don't switch off or "fix" it.
        No
        ▼
 Did you send money or data? ─────── Yes ──► Call [FINANCE CONTACT] and your bank at once.
        │
        No ───► Report it and relax. You did well.
```

## What to include
- The message itself (use *Forward as attachment* or the report button, so headers are kept).
- What you clicked or entered, and roughly when.
- Anything unusual since.

## Our promise
Reporting is **always welcome**. No blame, no punishment for honest mistakes, and you will get a reply.
