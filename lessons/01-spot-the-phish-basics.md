# Lesson 01 — Spot the phish: the basics (25 min)

**Audience:** all staff · **Format:** in person or video call · **Materials:** [`examples/01`](../examples/01-credential-phishing.md), [cheat sheet](../checklists/spot-a-phishing-email.md)

## Objectives
By the end, participants can:
1. Explain what phishing is and why it works.
2. Name the five common red flags.
3. State the safe default: *pause, verify, report*.

## Outline
| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 0–3 | Welcome. Set the tone: "Everyone gets fooled sometimes. Today is about skills, not blame." |
| 3–8 | What phishing is. Why it works: emotion (urgency, fear, curiosity), authority, routine. |
| 8–18 | Walk through example 01 using the numbered markers. Ask the group to call out flags first. |
| 18–23 | Introduce the cheat sheet. Pairs: find the flags in example 03. |
| 23–25 | Reporting: how, where, and that fast reporting is praised. |

## Talking points
- Attackers target emotions, not computers. Urgency is a signal.
- Check the **real** sender domain and the **real** link destination.
- When unsure, do not click: use a bookmark or call a known number.
- Reporting early limits damage. Nobody is punished for reporting, even after a click.

## Check for understanding
Ask: "What are the three things you do when a message feels off?" (Pause, verify through another channel, report.)
