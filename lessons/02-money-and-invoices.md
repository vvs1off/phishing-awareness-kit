# Lesson 02 — Money, invoices, and impersonation (30 min)

**Audience:** finance, procurement, assistants, managers · **Materials:** examples [02](../examples/02-invoice-bec-fraud.md), [09](../examples/09-executive-impersonation-gift-cards.md)

## Objectives
1. Recognize payment-diversion and executive-impersonation patterns.
2. Apply the "verify out-of-band" rule.
3. Know the escalation path if money has moved.

## Outline
| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 0–5 | Story: a fictional team pays an invoice to the wrong account. Cost of one email. |
| 5–15 | Example 02: spot the reply-to mismatch, the double extension, and the secrecy request. |
| 15–22 | Example 09: why gift cards and "discreet" tasks are warning signs. |
| 22–28 | Group exercise: write your team's verification rule on one slide. |
| 28–30 | Escalation: contact the bank at once, then security. |

## Talking points
- No bank-detail change from email alone. Always call a number **already on file**.
- Two-person approval for new payees and changed details.
- Secrecy and urgency together are the hallmark.
- Senior leaders should publicly say they welcome being verified.
