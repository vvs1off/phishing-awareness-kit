## 1. Purpose
To help everyone at [ORGANIZATION NAME] recognize and report phishing, and to build a culture where reporting is encouraged.

## 2. Scope
All employees, contractors, and volunteers who use [ORGANIZATION NAME] systems.

## 3. Expectations
1. Treat unexpected requests for credentials, codes, payments, or data with caution.
2. Verify sensitive requests through an independent, known channel.
3. Report suspicious messages promptly via [REPORT CHANNEL].
4. Complete awareness training: [FREQUENCY].

## 4. Our commitments
- Reporting in good faith, including after a mistake, will **not** lead to discipline.
- Training is respectful and practical; results are not used to shame individuals.
- Personal data collected for the program is limited, protected, and retained for [PERIOD].

## 5. Financial controls
- Changes to payee bank details require verification by phone using details already on file and approval by [TWO ROLES].
- Payments are not made on the basis of email instructions alone.

## 6. Awareness exercises (if any)
Any simulated-phishing exercises follow the [program guidance](../docs/running-an-ethical-program.md), with prior notice of the program, legal/HR sign-off, and no punitive use.
